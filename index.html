About

I'm a Supply Chain Management major at Rutgers Business School (Class of 2028) with a strong interest in the operations behind the products we buy. Every product on a shelf or a screen reflects careful planning, sourcing, and shipping, and I enjoy helping that process run smoothly.

I built on that interest last summer, managing product catalogs and inventory data across Amazon, Walmart, and eBay. Today I bring the same attention to detail to my role as Treasurer of CHAARG, where I plan events and manage the chapter budget, and to the other communities I'm part of on campus.

I'm seeking internships in supply chain, logistics, procurement, merchandising, and e-commerce in the New York City area. If you're building something where data, products, and people meet, I'd welcome the chance to connect.

Beyond the supply chain

A genuine passion for retail and fashion

I've always been drawn to how a brand builds its world and gets a collection to the right store at the right time. Inside LVMH let me explore both creation and branding and operations and supply chain, and it reinforced my belief that great products need great operations behind them.
